Head Master is the backbone of the society software.

Each head defines a General Ledger (GL) account and controls how it behaves — whether it appears in Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, or Balance Sheet, whether interest is calculated on it, and which module (Savings, FD, Loan, Pigmmy, etc.) handles accounts under it.

Column Legend

Int = Interest Given (Y/N) — whether interest is credited to accounts under this head
Txn = Allow Transactions (Y/N) — whether receipt/payment is allowed against this head
Report Position = Where this head appears: Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, or Trial Balance only
Balance = Current GL balance of this head, updated in real-time
