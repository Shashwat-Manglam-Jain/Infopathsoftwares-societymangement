What is this?

Recalculates member account balances (Savings, Current, Security, Loan) from their transaction history. Use this when an account's displayed balance doesn't match the sum of its transactions — caused by a failed operation, partial rollback, or data fix.

Example: Member's savings shows ₹10,000 but transaction total is ₹10,500. This tool recalculates and fixes it to ₹10,500.

Safe to run: Only accounts with mismatched balances are corrected. Accounts already correct are skipped.
