What is this?

Recalculates GL head (Chart of Accounts) balances from journal entry lines. Use this when the trial balance doesn't tally or a GL head's balance looks wrong compared to its ledger detail.

Example: "Savings Deposit" GL head shows ₹5,00,000 but journal entries total ₹5,02,000. This tool fixes it to ₹5,02,000 by recomputing from Opening Balance + all journal lines.

Safe to run: Only heads with mismatched balances are corrected. Correct heads are skipped.
