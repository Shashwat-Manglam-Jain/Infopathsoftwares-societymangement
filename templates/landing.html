Frequently Asked Questions

Is my society's data safe?

Absolutely. Your data is stored on AWS servers in Mumbai (India), encrypted with 256-bit SSL, and backed up every 6 hours. Each society has its own isolated database — no other society can see your data. We maintain a complete audit trail of every action.

Can I import existing member data from Excel?

Yes. You can import your member data from Excel/CSV files. We provide a template with the required columns. Upload your file and members are added instantly. You can also export all data to Excel anytime.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation needed. InfoSociety is fully cloud-based. Just open your browser, log in, and start working. Works on any computer, tablet, or phone. Updates are automatic — you always have the latest version.

What happens after the free trial?

After 90 days, choose a plan that fits your society. Your data stays as-is — nothing is lost. If you decide not to continue, you can export all your data before your account is deactivated.

Can I export my data?

Yes. All reports can be exported to PDF and Excel. You can download member lists, transaction history, account statements, and financial reports at any time. Your data belongs to you.
